FAQ

Good to know

Is it a private room?

Each guest has their own room, separated by walls and a curtain rather than a closing door. You will not share a space with other guests. Couples or friends who want to be side by side can ask when booking.

Will my hair be wet afterwards?

No. We wash, dry and style your hair at the end, so you can go straight to dinner or back to work.

Do you speak Japanese or English?

Our therapists speak Thai and basic English. Bookings on LINE can be made in Japanese, English or Thai, and the treatment itself needs no conversation.

Can I walk in without a booking?

Sometimes, but rooms are limited. Please message us on LINE or call before coming so we can hold a room for you.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please let us know at least 24 hours in advance if you need to change or cancel. Same-day cancellations and no-shows mean the room stays empty, so repeated no-shows may affect future bookings.

How can I pay?

Cash and Thai QR payment (PromptPay). Please ask on LINE if you need another method.
